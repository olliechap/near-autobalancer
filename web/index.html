Keep the light on

  1. Your vault is 20 crates of NEAR, 5,000 each. Crates by the lighthouse are staked for NEAR AI: they fill the credit tank that keeps the light on. Crates on the barge are staked for yield: they earn NEAR.
  2. Ships are your AI requests. If the tank runs dry the light goes out and your requests are throttled.
  3. Drag a crate across to move it. It rides the ferry for 4 epochs (28 hours) before it lands, so move early. Usage will spike at some point in the month.
  4. Score: NEAR earned, minus 2 for every hour throttled. At the end you see what the Ballast agent did with the same month.
  5. Keys: ← sends a crate to the lighthouse, → sends one to the barge, A lets the agent play, space pauses.
